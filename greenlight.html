01 / THE BRAND

What is GreenLight?

GreenLight Collectibles is a diecast model company known for licensed replicas of vehicles from automotive brands, popular culture and real-world fleets. Its catalogue includes passenger cars, trucks, emergency vehicles, movie and television vehicles, and other themed releases.

GreenLight offers models in multiple scales. The 1:64 range is especially familiar to small-scale collectors, while larger scales are also part of the brand's wider product range. Always check the scale on the exact package or product listing.

04 / SCALE & CRAFT

Understanding GreenLight scales

GreenLight makes models in more than one scale, so do not assume every GreenLight vehicle is 1:64. At 1:64, 64 units on the real vehicle are represented by one unit on the model. A 4,480 mm car would be about 70 mm long at an exact 1:64 scale.

Details to inspect

  • Scale printed on the package
  • Body shape and proportions
  • Wheel design and alignment
  • Paint, tampo and licence details
  • Interior, lights and windows
  • Base, packaging and release number

Why scale matters

Models in different scales will not sit at the same size in a display. If you are building a 1:64 collection or diorama, check the scale before buying, especially when shopping for trucks, emergency vehicles or larger subjects.

Construction materials and opening features can also vary between product lines.

05 / BEGINNER'S GUIDE

How to start collecting GreenLight

01

Pick a theme

Try movie cars, American classics, pickups, emergency vehicles or a particular scale.

02

Check the scale and series

Read the package or listing carefully. Note the vehicle, scale, series name and release number.

03

Inspect the condition

For carded models, check the card and blister. For boxed models, check the box, case and model itself.

Quick buying checklist
  • Is the scale clearly stated?
  • Does the listing show the exact vehicle and series?
  • Are packaging and accessories included?
  • Is the seller reputable and the return policy clear?
  • Have you compared prices for the same release?

06 / COLLECTOR KNOW-HOW

Rarity, value & care

Are GreenLight models all equally collectible?

No. Interest varies by vehicle, licence, series, release quantity, condition and collector demand. A chase or special edition may attract attention, but a special label alone does not guarantee high resale value.

What should you check on carded models?

Look at the card corners, edges, blister clarity and whether the model is securely held. If you prefer loose models, make sure the vehicle is complete and any small parts are present.

How should you store them?

Keep models away from moisture, direct sunlight and heavy dust. Store packaging flat or upright without crushing it, and use a stable display case for loose models.

07 / QUICK ANSWERS

GreenLight FAQs

Is GreenLight only a 1:64 brand?

No. GreenLight has products in multiple scales. Check the scale printed on the individual product or package.

Does GreenLight make movie and TV cars?

Yes, movie and television vehicle themes are part of its licensed product range, although availability depends on current and past releases.

What does a chase model mean?

Collectors use the term for a special variant that is distributed in lower quantities than a regular version. Exact definitions and availability depend on the particular series.

Are GreenLight models good for dioramas?

Many collectors use GreenLight vehicles in displays and dioramas. Check scale and proportions so the model fits the scene you are building.

What should a beginner buy first?

Choose a vehicle or theme you enjoy, confirm the scale and condition, and set a comfortable budget before purchasing.
