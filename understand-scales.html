01 / THE BASICS

What does scale mean?

Scale tells you how much smaller a model is compared with the real vehicle. The number after the colon is the factor by which each corresponding linear dimension is divided.

1:64

One to sixty-four

A 1:64 model is one sixty-fourth of the real vehicle's length, width and height, assuming it is made to an exact 1:64 scale.

Remember: A smaller denominator means a physically larger model. For example, a 1:18 model is larger than a 1:64 model of the same car.

04 / A COMMON QUESTION

Why aren't all 1:64 cars the same size?

Because the real vehicles are not all the same size. A full-size SUV is generally longer and taller than a small sports car, so their 1:64 replicas will also differ in size.

Collector tip

Don't judge a model's scale only by comparing its length with another model. Compare it with the dimensions of the real vehicle whenever accurate scale matters.

Also, some toy and collectible ranges use approximate scales rather than exact mathematical scaling. Check the manufacturer's stated scale when precision is important.

There is no single best scale. Choose based on the cars you like, your display space and your collecting budget.
