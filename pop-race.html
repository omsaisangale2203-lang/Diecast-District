01 / THE BRAND

What is Pop Race?

Pop Race is a modern diecast and scale-model label aimed at collectors. It is particularly visible in the 1:64 collecting scene, with releases that appeal to fans of Japanese cars, tuning culture and racing-inspired designs.

Its catalogue and release mix can change over time. Rather than treating every model as part of one fixed series, check the individual product listing for its scale, construction, edition, accessories and release details.

04 / SCALE & CRAFT

Understanding 1:64 scale

At 1:64 scale, every 64 units of real-world length become one unit on the model. For example, a real car measuring 4,480 mm long would measure about 70 mm at an exact 1:64 scale.

Details to inspect

  • Body shape and overall proportions
  • Wheel design and alignment
  • Paint finish and decal placement
  • Headlights, tail lamps and grille
  • Interior and window details
  • Base, packaging and model identification

Read the product description

Do not assume all releases use the same materials or construction. Check whether the specific model is diecast, resin or another type, and confirm whether a display case, base or accessories are included.

Opening parts, rolling wheels and display features can differ between individual models.

05 / BEGINNER'S GUIDE

How to start collecting Pop Race

01

Pick a collection theme

Choose a direction you enjoy, such as Japanese sports cars, modified street builds or motorsport designs.

02

Identify the exact release

Note the full model name, scale, colour, edition and any included display accessories.

03

Compare sellers

Look for clear photographs of the actual item, a detailed description, seller reputation and a clear return policy.

04

Store it carefully

Keep the model and its packaging away from moisture, dust, direct sunlight and places where it can be knocked over.

Quick buying checklist
  • Does the listing clearly state the scale?
  • Do the photos match the exact model and colour?
  • Are the box, case and accessories included?
  • Is the seller trustworthy and the return policy clear?
  • Have you compared prices for the same release?

06 / COLLECTOR KNOW-HOW

Rarity, value & model care

How do you judge a model's value?

Look at the exact release, condition, availability, demand and recent prices for comparable examples. A limited label or an expensive listing alone does not prove that a model is valuable. Avoid buying only because someone claims the price will rise.

How should you display your models?

Use a stable shelf or display case. Keep models away from direct sunlight, damp areas and heavy dust. Handle them carefully, preferably by the base where practical, and avoid harsh cleaners on paint, decals or clear parts.

Why keep the original packaging?

The box and inserts help identify the release and protect the model during storage or transport. Keep small accessories together so they do not get misplaced.

07 / QUICK ANSWERS

Pop Race FAQs

Is Pop Race a 1:64 brand?

Pop Race is widely associated with 1:64 scale collecting. Always confirm the scale on the individual product listing rather than assuming every product is the same size.

Does Pop Race make only Japanese cars?

No. Japanese automotive subjects are a visible part of its appeal, but check the current catalogue for the full range of manufacturers and themes.

Are all models diecast?

Do not assume that every release uses the same construction. Check the product details for the material and features of the exact model.

Are special editions always rare?

No. Rarity depends on actual production and availability, while value also depends on condition and collector demand.

What should a beginner buy first?

Start with a model you genuinely like and can comfortably afford. Learn to identify exact releases before spending extra on special variants.
