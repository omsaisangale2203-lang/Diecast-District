01 / THE BRAND

What is Para64?

Para64 is a name collectors encounter in the premium 1:64 scale model-car space. Its models are aimed at enthusiasts who enjoy compact replicas with carefully presented vehicle shapes, paint finishes and small-scale details.

As with any model brand, the range and specifications can vary by release. Check the exact product listing for the vehicle, scale, construction material, edition details and included display accessories.

04 / SCALE & CRAFT

Understanding 1:64 scale

At 1:64 scale, 64 units on the real vehicle are represented by one unit on the miniature. For example, a real car that is 4,480 mm long would be about 70 mm long at an exact 1:64 scale.

Details worth checking

  • Body shape and proportions
  • Wheel design and alignment
  • Paint finish and decal placement
  • Headlights, tail lamps and grille
  • Interior and window details
  • Base, packaging and model identification

Check the construction

Do not assume every release uses the same materials or features. Read the individual product description to confirm whether the model is diecast, resin or another construction type.

Opening parts, rolling wheels, display bases and protective cases can differ between models.

05 / BEGINNER'S GUIDE

How to start collecting Para64

01

Choose cars you like

Start with real vehicles that interest you. A collection feels more personal when it follows your taste.

02

Check the exact model

Confirm the vehicle name, scale, colour, edition and construction details before placing an order.

03

Compare sellers

Look for clear photographs of the actual item, a complete description, seller reputation and a reasonable return policy.

04

Plan your display

Choose a stable shelf or case and leave enough room to handle models without knocking them over.

Quick buying checklist
  • Is the scale clearly stated?
  • Do the photographs match the exact colour and release?
  • Are the box, case and accessories included as advertised?
  • Is the seller trustworthy and the return policy clear?
  • Have you compared prices for the same model?

06 / COLLECTOR KNOW-HOW

Rarity, value & model care

Does a limited release always mean high value?

No. Value depends on the specific model, availability, condition, authenticity and collector demand. A limited label or a high asking price does not automatically make a model rare or a good investment.

How should you store your models?

Keep models away from direct sunlight, moisture and heavy dust. Use a stable display case or shelf and handle models carefully, preferably by the base where practical.

Why keep the original packaging?

Original packaging and inserts help identify the release and protect the model during storage or transport. Keep any small accessories together.

07 / QUICK ANSWERS

Para64 FAQs

Is Para64 a 1:64 scale brand?

Para64 is associated with the 1:64 scale model-car market. Confirm the scale on the exact product listing before purchasing.

Are all Para64 models made from the same material?

Do not assume so. Check the individual release details for its construction material and features.

Are special editions always rare?

No. Actual availability, condition and collector demand matter more than a special-edition label alone.

What should a beginner buy first?

Choose a model you genuinely like and can comfortably afford. Learn to identify exact releases before paying extra for special variants.

How can I avoid buying the wrong version?

Compare the full product name, colour, photos, packaging and included accessories. Ask the seller for actual-item photos if the listing is unclear.
