01 / IDENTIFY YOUR MODEL

Tell us about the car.

Upload a photo, enter the details, or do both. A photo helps with visual reference but does not automatically verify the exact variant.

Photo tip

Use a clear side or three-quarter view. If possible, include the base, packaging, model name or product code in another photo when checking a specific variant.

The first version displays your photo locally; it does not send it to an image-recognition service.

02 / MARKET EVIDENCE

Compare like with like.

Enter prices from similar models in the same variant and condition. Prefer completed sales over seller asking prices.

A rarity label is only as reliable as its evidence. Do not select limited, chase or discontinued unless you have verified it from a trustworthy source.

    COLLECTOR'S RULE

    Rare doesn't always mean valuable.

    A model can be limited but have low demand. A common casting can become desirable. Judge the deal using exact-variant comparisons, condition, demand and credible completed sales—not hype or one unusually high listing.
