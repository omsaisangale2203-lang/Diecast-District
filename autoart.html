01 / THE BRAND

What is AUTOart?

AUTOart is a scale-model brand known for highly detailed replicas of production cars, performance vehicles, supercars and motorsport subjects. Its models are designed primarily for display collectors who appreciate accurate proportions, carefully finished surfaces and intricate small details.

AUTOart has offered models in several scales, with 1:18 being a particularly recognisable part of its range. Product construction and features can differ by model and series, so always check the exact listing for scale, material and opening parts.

04 / SCALE & CRAFT

Understanding AUTOart scales and materials

AUTOart has produced models in more than one scale. At 1:18, one unit on the model represents 18 units on the real vehicle. A real car that is 4,500 mm long would be about 250 mm long at an exact 1:18 scale.

Details to inspect

  • Body shape and proportions
  • Paint finish and panel alignment
  • Interior, dashboard and seats
  • Wheels, tyres, brakes and suspension details
  • Lights, badges and exterior trim
  • Packaging, base and included accessories

Diecast and composite

AUTOart has used different construction approaches across its range, including diecast and composite models. Composite is a construction category, not a scale.

Check the product description for the material, opening parts and handling guidance for the exact model.

How to start collecting AUTOart

  • Is the scale clearly stated?

  • Are the box, base and accessories included?
  • Are condition, seller reputation and return terms clear?

06 / COLLECTOR KNOW-HOW

Value, condition & care

Are all AUTOart models investments?

No. Collect models because you enjoy the vehicles and craftsmanship. Resale prices can change with demand, availability, condition and the exact release; a high asking price does not guarantee future value.

What should you inspect before buying?

Look for paint marks, broken mirrors, loose parts, damaged wheels, missing accessories and packaging condition. Ask for clear photographs of the actual model, including details that are hidden in promotional images.

How should you care for larger models?

Use a stable shelf or display case, avoid direct sunlight and moisture, and lift the model carefully from a solid part of its base or chassis. Small mirrors, spoilers and other details may be delicate.

07 / QUICK ANSWERS

AUTOart FAQs

Is AUTOart mainly a 1:18 brand?

1:18 is a prominent scale in AUTOart's collector range, but the brand has offered models in other scales too. Check the exact product listing.

What does composite mean?

It refers to a model construction approach using composite materials. It is not a scale. Check the product description for the specific materials and features.

Do all AUTOart models have opening doors and hoods?

No. Opening features vary by model and series. Confirm them in the individual product specifications.

Are AUTOart models suitable for beginners?

They can be, provided the collector checks the price, scale, material, condition and available display space before buying.

How do I protect delicate details?

Handle the model gently, avoid lifting it by mirrors or spoilers, and keep it in a stable display area away from dust and accidental knocks.
