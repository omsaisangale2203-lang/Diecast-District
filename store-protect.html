01 / START WITH THE BASICS

Good care starts with good habits.

A diecast model can collect dust, scratches and signs of wear if it is handled or stored carelessly. A simple routine—clean hands, a suitable display area and careful handling—can help keep your collection looking its best.

The golden rule

Handle models gently, keep them away from avoidable hazards, and choose storage that suits the model and its packaging.

02 / DISPLAY & STORAGE

Choose the right home for your models.

01

Acrylic display cases

Help reduce dust and accidental contact while keeping models visible. Choose a case with enough room so cars do not rub against one another.

02

Display shelves

Make collections easy to see and organise. Use stable shelves and avoid overcrowding or placing models near edges.

03

Storage boxes

Useful for models not currently on display. Use clean, dry containers and separate models so they do not knock together.

04

Individual protectors

Protective cases can help prevent scratches and pressure on carded or loose models. Select a protector made for the model's dimensions.

Before placing a model in any case or box, make sure it is clean and completely dry. Avoid forcing a model into a tight space.

03 / CARDED & LOOSE

Different models need different care.

CARDED MODELS

Protect the packaging too.

  • Store cards flat or upright in a way that prevents bending.
  • Use correctly sized protectors where appropriate.
  • Avoid pressure on blister packs and card corners.
  • Keep packaging away from moisture and direct sunlight.
LOOSE MODELS

Protect the miniature itself.

  • Pick up models gently, avoiding unnecessary pressure on small parts.
  • Give each model enough space in a display or storage tray.
  • Keep loose accessories together in a labelled container.
  • Return models to a safe place after handling.

04 / CLEANING & HANDLING

Keep it clean. Keep it gentle.

01Use a clean, soft microfiber cloth to gently remove loose surface dust.
02For tight areas, use a very soft, clean brush without pressing hard.
03Do not use household cleaners, strong solvents or abrasive materials on paint, decals or plastic parts.
04If you are unsure how a finish or material will react, test only with manufacturer-approved care guidance—or leave it untouched.
Avoid risky cleaning experiments

Do not soak a model or apply chemicals just because they worked on another item. Different paints, decals, plastics and metal finishes can react differently.

05 / THE RIGHT ENVIRONMENT

Protect your collection from its surroundings.

☀

Direct sunlight

Long exposure can fade colours and affect some plastics and packaging. Display models away from strong direct sunlight.

💧

Moisture

Damp conditions can damage cardboard packaging and may contribute to corrosion on metal parts. Keep storage dry.

〰

Heat

Avoid hot windowsills, heaters and enclosed places that become very warm. Heat can affect some plastic components and packaging.

✦

Dust & contact

Use suitable cases or gentle regular dusting. Keep models away from busy edges and places where they may be knocked over.
