01 / THE BRAND

What is Solido?

Solido is a historic French model brand known for diecast vehicles. Its story stretches back to the early twentieth century, and its catalogue has included miniature cars, trucks, military models and other vehicle subjects.

Today, Solido is familiar to collectors for accessible display models in scales such as 1:18 and 1:43, with a range that includes classic vehicles, modern sports cars and motorsport subjects. Always check the exact product listing for scale, construction and features.

04 / SCALE & CRAFT

Understanding Solido scales

Solido is well known for scales including 1:18 and 1:43. At 1:18, a real car measuring 4,500 mm long would be about 250 mm long as a model. At 1:43, the same car would be about 105 mm long.

Details to inspect

  • Scale and product reference
  • Body shape and proportions
  • Paint finish and panel alignment
  • Wheels, tyres and brake details
  • Interior, lights and window trim
  • Base, packaging and included accessories

Features can vary

Opening doors, hoods, steering and other functions depend on the individual model. Read the product specifications rather than assuming every Solido release has the same features.

Construction materials and display accessories can also differ by scale and series.

06 / COLLECTOR KNOW-HOW

Value, condition & care

Are older Solido models always valuable?

No. Age alone does not determine value. Condition, exact version, packaging, availability and collector demand all matter. Compare the same model reference and scale when checking prices.

What should you inspect before buying?

Check for paint marks, missing mirrors, damaged wheels, loose parts, broken aerials or spoilers, and packaging condition. Ask for actual-item photographs if the listing is unclear.

How should you care for larger models?

Display them on a stable shelf, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Handle them carefully and avoid lifting them by mirrors, spoilers or other small parts.

Solido FAQs

Is Solido a French brand?

Yes. Solido has French origins and a long history in model vehicle production.

What scales is Solido known for?

Its modern collector range is familiar in scales such as 1:18 and 1:43. Check the exact product listing for scale.

Does every Solido model have opening parts?

No. Opening features and other functions vary by individual model and series.

Does Solido make rally cars?

Rally and motorsport subjects are part of its model range, though availability changes over time.

What should a beginner buy first?

Choose a scale and vehicle theme you enjoy, confirm the model's condition and features, and stay within a comfortable budget.
